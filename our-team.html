The Core Movement
"My dream is not just to build a successful agricultural company. It is to create a movement where farmers become entrepreneurs, the next generation becomes a force of innovation, agriculture becomes a source of opportunity, and rural communities become centres of prosperity."
Collective Strength • Shared Prosperity — Durgesh Chaudhari, Chairman
format_quote

"At Girna Global Farmer Producer Company Limited, I strongly believe that agriculture is not merely a source of livelihood; it is the foundation of our economy, our culture, and our future."

My vision behind Girna Global is to create an organisation built on the collective strength, aspirations, and hard work of farmers. Rooted in the fertile soil of Maharashtra, we aspire to cultivate prosperity from the grassroots and establish a meaningful connection between local agricultural strength and global opportunities.

I believe that farmers should not remain limited to agricultural production alone. They deserve to become active partners in value creation, business development, decision-making, and economic growth. Our objective is to empower farmers through collective efforts, modern technology, innovation, knowledge, and access to global markets.

I strongly believe that the future of Indian agriculture depends on embracing modern technology, scientific farming practices, sustainable resource management, and continuous innovation. We aim to promote agricultural excellence while protecting our soil, conserving water, and preserving the natural resources that sustain future generations.

school
Transforming the Next Generation

One of my most important dreams is to transform the mindset and opportunities of the next generation of farming families. I want young people from agricultural backgrounds to look beyond conventional employment and develop the confidence, knowledge, and capabilities to become entrepreneurs, innovators, and future business leaders. I envision a generation that creates employment opportunities rather than simply searches for jobs.

For us, growth is meaningful only when it is shared. I believe the success of Girna Global should not be measured merely through turnover, export volumes, or business expansion, but through the prosperity of our member farmers, the development of rural communities, and the opportunities we create for generations to come.

Our journey will extend from strengthening farmers at the grassroots to reaching international markets, from agricultural production to value addition, and from traditional farming to modern, sustainable, and globally competitive agricultural enterprises.

While our vision is global, our roots will always remain connected to the soil of Maharashtra.
